About us

ALMINGO S.L. is a company that develops software and apps for companies and individuals.

We have a new management team. They want to change our computer systems and databases, and they want to reach the international market. That is why this website is in English.

Sustainability Bingo

Our first project with the new management is a collective bingo. Everyone plays together in one room and learns about sustainability while having fun.

Learn

Discover simple ideas to protect the environment.

Play

Compete with your colleagues to complete a line.

Win

The winner explains the word to everyone.

How to play

  1. Take a card with sustainability words.
  2. Listen to the clue from the host.
  3. Cross out the word on your card.
  4. Complete a line and shout "Bingo!"
