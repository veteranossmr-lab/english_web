About us

ALMINGO S.L. is an innovative company committed to developing software, applications, and digital solutions for both businesses and individuals. Our mission is to combine technology, creativity, and sustainability to deliver efficient and environmentally responsible products.

Recently, a new management team joined the company with a clear vision for the future. Their goal is to modernise our digital infrastructure, improve our technological capabilities, and expand ALMINGO's presence beyond national borders. As part of this international growth strategy, our website is now available in English.

Sustainability is one of our core values. We strive to reduce our environmental impact by promoting paperless processes, optimising energy consumption in our digital operations, and supporting responsible business practices. We believe that technological innovation and environmental responsibility should go hand in hand.

This new website will help us connect with customers around the world, while our renewed leadership and commitment to sustainable development will allow us to build a greener and more innovative future for our clients, partners, and communities.
